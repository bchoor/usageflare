Setup

Your Worker, your accounts, read-only.

usageflare is a Hono app on Cloudflare Workers. It lives in your account and never writes to the ones it watches.

Step 1

Deploy the Worker

One Worker and one KV namespace. Put it behind Cloudflare Access so only your team can open it.

Step 2

Add a token per account

Create a read-only API token in each account and paste it in Settings. It's encrypted with AES-GCM under SETTINGS_KEY and never sent back out.

Step 3

Read it at a glance

usageflare pulls Cloudflare GraphQL analytics, matches each metric to your plan, and forecasts it to the end of the billing period.
